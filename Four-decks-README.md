## Selection and dates

Four distinct recent competitive sample decklists, not an asserted rank 1-4 or exhaustive November meta ranking. Latest accessible official Standard League collection published October30, event October25. Tokyo Standard championship October18 is corroborating earlier evidence.

These are exact October 25 League Season 3 team-event recipes, published October30. The event has a special maximum3 copies per card across4 teammates. They are current competitive examples, not a global tier ranking or independently optimized 1on1 builds.

As-of date: 2026-11-15. All61 original database entries have blockA. Product releases and banned/restricted-list snapshots were checked; unreleased Nov21 Eternal products were excluded.

61 unique cards / 160 physical copies across4 decks / 40 per deck.

Every generated card must be treated as a visual mockup. Generation can reproduce fine artwork/type imperfectly. Reviewed English rules and exact cost/BP/Core data remain available in the catalog and JSON, together with untouched original images and source hashes.
